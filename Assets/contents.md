## Section 4 — Solstice Story (Chapter 07)

**Frame:** `3rd.jpg` — three Solstice GTs (silver, crimson, blue) driving toward camera on an open highway at dusk, headlights on. *(Design note: this frame stacks two photos — a dusk road shot above, the three-car shot below, with a soft fade at the seam so the cut between them reads as one continuous scene.)*

**Eyebrow:** THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

**Headline:** Named for the Longest Light

**Body:** The solstice marks the point where light lingers longest before the turn. It's a fitting name for a car designed to extend the moment behind the wheel — where the drive itself becomes the destination. Dusk, an open road, and the horizon still glowing: this is the hour the Solstice GT was built for. Three years of design iteration, wind-tunnel testing, and track validation went into a single goal: make the ordinary drive feel occasional.

**Pull Quote:** *(now set on its own dark backing panel — previously floated directly on the photo with no scrim)* "We didn't want to build a faster car. We wanted to build a car that made you want to drive slower, longer." — VELOR Design Lead (sample attribution)
