**Eyebrow:** FORM FOLLOWS VELOCITY

**Headline:**
A Silhouette Cut From Motion Itself

**Body:**
The Solstice GT's profile was shaped in a wind tunnel before it was ever sketched on paper. Every line — from the low, cab-forward roofline to the sculpted rear haunches and the carbon side intakes behind each door — exists because air demanded it. Crowned by a fixed carbon-fiber rear wing, the result is a shape that looks fast standing still.

- 21-inch forged alloy wheels (sample spec)
- Three signature finishes: Crimson Red, Storm Silver, Cobalt Blue

**Body:**

- Forged 21-inch alloy wheels with center V cap
- Michelin Pilot Sport 4 S tires (265/30 R21, sample spec)
- Six-piston red calipers with drilled carbon-ceramic rotors

## Section 4 — Solstice Story (Chapter 07)

**Frame (4.5 image):** `3rd.jpg` — three Solstice GTs (silver, crimson, blue) driving toward camera on an open highway at dusk, headlights on.

**Eyebrow:** THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

**Headline:**
Named for the Longest Light

**Body:**
The solstice marks the point where light lingers longest before the turn. It's a fitting name for a car designed to extend the moment behind the wheel — where the drive itself becomes the destination. Dusk, an open road, and the horizon still glowing: this is the hour the Solstice GT was built for. Three years of design iteration, wind-tunnel testing, and track validation went into a single goal: make the ordinary drive feel occasional.

**Pull Quote (sample):**
